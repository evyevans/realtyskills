# Example: a transaction milestone update

**Skill:** [Transaction Update Starter](../skills/transactions-and-documents/transaction-client-comms/SKILL.md)

## Input

```text
Client alias: Jordan.
Location: Ontario, Canada.
Stage: buyer has received a home inspection report.
Verified facts: report is available; agent has not reviewed it with the buyer.
Requested next step: offer a 20-minute call tomorrow afternoon.
No signed contract deadlines or inspection conclusions supplied.
Tone: calm, clear, concise.
Output: one email draft. Do not send it.
```

## Illustrative output

**Subject:** Let's review your inspection report

Hi Jordan,

Your inspection report is available. Let's review the findings together and identify any questions for the inspector or other professionals before deciding on next steps.

Would you be available for a 20-minute call tomorrow afternoon? Send me a time that works for you, and I'll confirm our appointment.

[Agent name]

## Review

The message does not declare the inspection satisfactory, invent repair needs, or create a contract deadline. Confirm actual deadlines from the signed documents before adding them. A draft suggesting a call does not book the call or send the email.
