# Example: listing copy from verified facts

**Skill:** [Listing Content Starter](../skills/listings-and-marketing/listing-description-engine/SKILL.md)

## Input

```text
Country and region: Canada; Toronto, Ontario.
Property: fictional 2-bedroom, 2-bath condo.
Size: 900 sq ft.
Verified features: balcony, one parking space, in-unit laundry.
Price: CAD 650,000.
Tone: factual and welcoming.
Output: listing description under 1,000 characters and one social caption.
No confirmed details about schools, transit, views, upgrades, or accessibility.
Use the provided facts only; ask about missing details if essential.
```

## Illustrative output

**Listing draft**

Two bedrooms, two bathrooms, and a private balcony in this 900 sq ft Toronto condo. In-unit laundry and one parking space add everyday convenience. Offered at CAD 650,000. Contact the listing representative for property details and showing information.

**Social caption**

Toronto condo listing: 2 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, 900 sq ft, a balcony, in-unit laundry, and one parking space. Asking CAD 650,000. Get in touch for verified property details and showing availability.

## Review

The drafts use only supplied features. They do not invent neighborhood benefits, renovation dates, fees, or showing dates. Confirm all facts, the brokerage's requirements, and the publishing platform's rules before posting. The sample price is fictional and does not represent a valuation.
