I’m open-sourcing RealtySkills: a free library of practical AI workflows for real estate professionals.

As an AI engineer, I wanted to make useful instructions easier for agents to find and use in their everyday work.

The library includes 42 workflows across 9 categories, with 12 starters for quick drafting tasks and 30 detailed playbooks for structured work.

Three ways to try it:

- Turn verified property details into listing copy and social captions.
- Prepare a seller call or a thoughtful lead follow-up sequence.
- Evaluate rental cash flow with explicit assumptions and checkable calculations.

You can copy or attach the instructions to an AI chat, or install individual skills in supported agent tools. The README walks through a first task without coding.

The library is free under the MIT License, with clear labels for workflows that use US-specific rules. AI outputs still need review, especially for legal, financial, and compliance tasks.

Explore RealtySkills: https://github.com/evyevans/realtyskills

If you find it useful, star the repository and share it with a colleague. I’d welcome feedback from agents: which task would you like covered next?
