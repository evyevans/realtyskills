# Connect skills into a real estate workflow

Run one skill at a time. Review its result, then provide the verified parts as input to the next skill. A chain of instructions does not create integrations or permission to act externally.

## Launch a listing

1. [Listing Appointment Starter](../skills/market-research-and-pricing/cma-listing-appointment-prep/SKILL.md): supply property facts and dated comparable sales. Review the pricing narrative and appointment agenda.
2. [Listing Content Starter](../skills/listings-and-marketing/listing-description-engine/SKILL.md): provide the confirmed facts, approved price, brand voice, and publishing limits. Review the listing copy and captions.
3. [Housing Marketing Risk Review](../skills/compliance-and-risk/fair-housing-compliance-checker/SKILL.md): review the draft against the applicable jurisdiction. The source baseline is US-specific; use current local guidance elsewhere.
4. [Listing Launch Plan](../skills/listings-and-marketing/listing-launch-coordinator/SKILL.md): use approved copy, launch date, budget, and vendor availability to build a checklist. You approve and publish the final assets.

## Qualify and follow up with leads

1. [Lead Qualification](../skills/leads-and-prospecting/lead-qualification-engine/SKILL.md): supply a redacted lead batch with source, readiness, timeline, engagement, and contact preferences. Review the heuristic scores and missing information.
2. [Lead Nurture Starter](../skills/client-communication-and-referrals/lead-nurture-machine/SKILL.md): choose one contactable lead, supply their confirmed goals and consent context, and draft the next touches.
3. [Objection Response Starter](../skills/client-communication-and-referrals/objection-handler/SKILL.md): provide the client's actual response and prepare a respectful reply. Review before sending.

An opt-out overrides a score: do not enroll someone who asked to stop contact in a campaign. Scores organize agent time; they do not determine housing eligibility.

## Coordinate a transaction

1. [Transaction Timeline](../skills/transactions-and-documents/transaction-coordinator-timeline/SKILL.md): supply signed contract deadlines and responsible parties. Confirm the calendar against the signed documents.
2. [Transaction Update Starter](../skills/transactions-and-documents/transaction-client-comms/SKILL.md): turn a verified milestone into a clear client update.
3. [Contract Review](../skills/transactions-and-documents/contract-review-and-red-flag-assistant/SKILL.md): prepare questions for qualified local professionals about clauses or missing information.
4. [Closing Document Review](../skills/transactions-and-documents/closing-document-reviewer/SKILL.md): reconcile supplied closing figures and flag questions. The source uses US closing documents; local forms need local professional review.

## Evaluate a rental acquisition

1. [Neighborhood Investment Report](../skills/market-research-and-pricing/neighborhood-investment-climate-report/SKILL.md): supply dated local evidence or enable browsing. Verify factual claims and data sources.
2. [Rental Property Evaluation](../skills/deals-and-investment-analysis/rental-property-underwriting-model/SKILL.md): provide price, verified rents, expenses, vacancy, reserves, and financing. Check the calculations and test downside assumptions.
3. [Transaction Risk Review](../skills/compliance-and-risk/transaction-risk-screener/SKILL.md): compile unresolved questions and missing documents for due diligence.
4. [Maintenance Work Order](../skills/property-management/property-management-work-order-tracker/SKILL.md): after acquisition, turn a verified maintenance request into a draft work order. Dispatch remains a separate authorized action.

For a worked financial example, see [rental evaluation](../examples/rental-evaluation.md).
