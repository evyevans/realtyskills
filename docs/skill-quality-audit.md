# Individual skill revision audit

All 42 active catalog entries were reviewed individually. The 35 remaining workflows were revised; the seven earlier revisions were rechecked and updated for consistency. This record describes instruction review and scenario walkthrough coverage, not recorded live-model results or certification across AI products.

## Review standard

Each workflow specifies essential inputs, domain procedure, evidence and uncertainty treatment, available-tool execution, complete chat fallback, concrete deliverables, and relevant action boundaries. Fixed fictional cases and unsupported market defaults were removed from active instructions. Legitimate formulas and applicable sourced rules remain. Historical examples and archive entries are labeled separately.

## Current official-source checks

Official starting points were checked during the revision. Future executions must recheck applicable changing rules; this record is not a permanently current legal summary.

- [IRS Publication 544](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p544) and [Form 8824 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8824): U.S. exchange timing and identification scope.

- [HUD rights and obligations](https://www.hud.gov/stat/fheo/rights-obligations): applicable housing-rights context.
- [FTC commercial-email guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business): commercial-email purpose, sender/opt-out requirements, and jurisdiction distinction.
- [Fannie Mae comparable-sale adjustments](https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b4-1.3-09/adjustments-comparable-sales): evidence-supported adjustment methodology; no universal adjustment cap was imported.
- [NAR written buyer agreements](https://www.nar.realtor/the-facts/written-buyer-agreements-101): applicable association/MLS context and actual negotiated terms, distinct from local law.
- [FinCEN current residential-rule FAQs](https://www.fincen.gov/rre-faqs): changes and court orders make current status checking essential; no fixed reporting mandate was retained.
