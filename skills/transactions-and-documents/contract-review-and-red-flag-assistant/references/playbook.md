# Contract Review — detailed procedure

## Document completeness

Identify title, form/version, parties and roles, execution status, pages, attachments, amendments, and incorporated terms. Note illegible scans and OCR uncertainty; request the specific missing page rather than reconstructing it. Do not assume a draft is executed or that a later file overrides a signed amendment. Resolve conflicts using the document's actual hierarchy where clear; otherwise flag for counsel.

## Extract the agreement

Map purchase/lease price, deposits and custodians, financing, inspection/diligence, appraisal, title/survey, disclosures, completion/possession, taxes and costs, assignment, notices, default/remedies, dispute resolution, and special provisions as applicable. Record section/page and exact operative language for each material finding. Do not demand irrelevant clauses merely because they are common elsewhere.

For every deadline record triggering event, period, calendar/business-day convention, inclusion/exclusion of trigger day, holidays/weekends, time zone, cutoff, notice method, responsible party, and evidence of trigger. Distinguish options, proposed dates, and binding obligations. Unclear rules produce a calculation question, not a confident deadline.

## Evaluate issues

Use severity based on plausible consequence and urgency, not an invented calibrated risk score. Findings should contain evidence, what is inconsistent or unresolved, the possible operational effect, confidence, missing facts, and a verification/action question. Separate ordinary negotiable terms from contradictions or potentially unlawful provisions. Do not certify enforceability, declare fraud, or state a breach occurred from a missing document alone.

Review deposit refund/forfeiture conditions, how contingencies are satisfied or waived, termination delivery, title objections, financing and appraisal interaction, possession/rent-back obligations, escalation caps, and cost allocation where present. “As is” does not by itself establish inspection, disclosure, or termination rights; examine the actual clauses and applicable law. Do not assume cash or hard-money financing means all protections are waived.

## Negotiation and handoff

Offer user-requested alternatives as proposals with tradeoffs and required party approval. Do not recommend waivers reflexively or guarantee a counterparty response. Address local form, statutory, and professional-practice questions using current applicable sources. If legal verification is unavailable, still summarize the evidence and draft precise counsel questions.

## Deliverable

Return document-set status; plain-language deal/obligation summary; issue table with page/section, evidence, consequence, urgency, and owner; deadline/notice matrix; financial/term inconsistencies; missing evidence; optional proposed negotiation language separate from original text; and prioritized broker/attorney questions. No universal minimum inspection period, deposit percentage, or closing window should replace negotiated terms.

## Before delivery

Every material finding has a clause or missing-document basis. Reconcile amendments and money totals, distinguish signed versus proposed terms, and do not describe an unchecked right as guaranteed.
