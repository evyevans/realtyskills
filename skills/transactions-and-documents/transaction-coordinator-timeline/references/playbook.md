# Transaction Timeline — detailed procedure

## Source extraction

For every obligation record document/page/section, responsible party, triggering event, evidence and timestamp, duration or fixed date, counting method, cutoff/time zone, notice method, dependencies, and completion evidence. Sources may include amendments, settlement/lender requirements, or verified applicable rules; indicate which source governs each item. Do not assume execution is always Day 0 or that every period begins at signing.

## Deadline calculation

Read the actual definition of calendar/business days and how weekends/holidays affect expiry. Business days are not universally weekdays; some rules use different definitions. Do not automatically move a deadline forward or apply a national holiday calendar without source support. Resolve numeric-date ambiguity and local time-zone interpretation before calculating. An unconfirmed delivery trigger makes the date provisional.

For applicable U.S. mortgage Closing Disclosure timing, check the actual coverage, receipt/delivery rule, definition of business day, and lender confirmation. Not every correction restarts a waiting period. Do not assert automatic rights loss, invalid contract, breach, or extension merely because a milestone is late; derive the consequence from the actual agreement and qualified review.

Use calculator/code when available to verify dates and show the counting rule. Ask for material gaps and deliver the remainder of the timeline rather than invent standard deposit, appraisal, financing, or inspection periods.

## Coordination

Build rows for deposit receipt, inspections and responses, financing/appraisal, title and objection periods, disclosures, insurance, settlement funding, walkthrough, possession, and other actual obligations. Omit irrelevant milestones. Owners must be user-provided roles or explicitly proposed assignments. A due task is not evidence it has been completed.

Identify critical dependencies and collisions using consequences and available lead time, without a fixed number of “highest-risk” deadlines. Add proposed reminders using user-approved buffers and availability. If closing or a trigger changes, recompute only dependent dates under the agreement; do not assume unrelated obligations move automatically.

## Deliverable

Return source/trigger register; calculated deadline worksheet; chronological timeline with owner, status, dependency, action, and evidence; unresolved calculation questions; immediate and near-term tasks; and a minimal contact directory. Include review drafts for necessary coordination messages if requested. With an authorized calendar tool, verify event time zone, titles, privacy, and creation result; otherwise provide a copy-ready table or calendar-import artifact if file generation is available.

## Before delivery

All binding dates trace to a clause/rule and confirmed event; time zones/counting definitions are explicit; planning reminders are separate; amended events update only dependent obligations.

## Official starting points

[CFPB Closing Disclosure timing](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/when-do-i-get-a-closing-disclosure-en-179/) and [TRID FAQs](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/compliance/compliance-resources/mortgage-resources/tila-respa-integrated-disclosures/tila-respa-integrated-disclosure-faqs/) for covered U.S. loans. Verify applicability and specific counting/receipt rules.

These are jurisdiction-specific starting points, not universal rules or proof of current applicability. Open and verify the relevant source during use; choose the corresponding local authority elsewhere.
