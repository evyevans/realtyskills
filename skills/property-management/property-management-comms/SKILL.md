- Confirm the country and state or province when contracts, taxes, housing, tenancy, licensing, or outreach rules affect the task. US examples do not establish rules elsewhere.

# Property Management Comms

Every communication template a property manager or rental investor needs to run a professional, legally-aware operation. From welcoming new tenants to handling the hardest conversations — late rent, lease violations, evictions — this skill produces clear, professional, and legally neutral language that protects the owner while treating tenants with respect.

## When to Use

- Property management companies handling day-to-day tenant communication
- Real estate investors who self-manage their rental portfolio
- Real estate attorneys drafting or reviewing standard PM communications
- Brokerage property management divisions

---

## ⚠️ Legal Disclaimer

Templates in this skill are general starting points. **Landlord-tenant law is highly state and city specific.** Notice periods, required language, security deposit rules, and eviction procedures vary significantly by jurisdiction. Always have a local real estate attorney review formal notices (eviction, non-renewal, security deposit disposition) before serving them. This skill does not constitute legal advice.

## TENANT COMMUNICATIONS

### 1 — Welcome / Move-In Letter

> **[Property Management Company / Landlord Name]**
> **[Date]**
>
> Dear [Tenant Name(s)],
>
> Welcome to your new home at [Property Address, Unit #]! We're glad to have you as a resident and want to make sure your move-in experience is as smooth as possible.
>
> **Your Lease Details:**
> • Lease Start Date: [Date]
> • Monthly Rent: $[Amount]
> • Rent Due Date: [Day of month] of each month
> • Rent Payment Method: [Check / ACH / Online portal at URL]
> • Security Deposit Held: $[Amount]
>
> **Important Contacts:**
> • Maintenance Requests: [Phone / Email / Portal URL]
> • Emergency Maintenance (after hours): [Phone]
> • Office / Leasing: [Phone] | [Email]
>
> **Move-In Checklist:**
> Please complete the enclosed Move-In Condition Report within [3/5] days and return a signed copy to us. This protects both you and us when it comes time to move out.
>
> **A Few Key Points:**
> • Rent received after [day] of the month is subject to a late fee of $[Amount]
> • All maintenance requests should be submitted in writing via [method]
> • Please review your lease for guest policy, pet policy, and parking terms
>
> We're here to make this a great experience. Don't hesitate to reach out.
>
> Sincerely,
> [Property Manager Name]
> [Company] | [Phone] | [Email]

### 3 — Late Rent Notice — Day 1 (Friendly)

**Text:**
> Hi [Tenant Name], this is [Manager] at [Property]. We noticed your rent for [Month] hasn't been received yet. If there's an issue, please reach out today so we can discuss options. Late fees apply after [day]. Thanks.

### 4 — Late Rent Notice — Day 5 (Formal)

> **NOTICE OF OVERDUE RENT**
>
> Date: [Date]
> To: [Tenant Name(s)]
> Property: [Address, Unit #]
>
> As of today, your rent payment of $[Amount] for the period of [Month] remains outstanding. This payment was due on [Due Date].
>
> **Current Balance Due:**
> Rent: $[Amount]
> Late Fee: $[Amount]
> **Total Due: $[Total]**
>
> Please remit payment in full immediately via [payment method]. If full payment is not received by [Date], we will be required to proceed in accordance with your lease agreement and applicable law.
>
> If you are experiencing a financial hardship, please contact our office immediately at [Phone] to discuss your options.
>
> [Property Manager Name]
> [Company] | [Phone]

---

### 5 — Late Rent Notice — Day 10+ (Pre-Legal / Pay or Quit)

> **NOTICE TO PAY RENT OR QUIT**
> *(Consult local attorney before serving — notice period and required language vary by state)*
>
> Date: [Date]
> To: [Tenant Name(s)]
> Premises: [Full Property Address, Unit #]
>
> You are hereby notified that as of [Date], you are in default of your lease agreement dated [Lease Date] for failure to pay rent. The following amounts are past due:
>
> Rent ([Month]): $[Amount]
> Late Fees: $[Amount]
> Other Charges: $[Amount]
> **Total Due: $[Total]**
>
> YOU ARE REQUIRED TO EITHER:
> (1) Pay the total amount due of $[Amount] within [X] days of receipt of this notice, OR
> (2) Vacate and surrender possession of the premises located at [Address].
>
> If you fail to pay or vacate within the time specified, legal proceedings may be initiated to recover possession of the premises and all amounts owed.
>
> [Property Manager / Landlord Name]
> [Date]
> [Phone]
>
> *This notice has been served on [Date] by [method of service].*

>

>
> Your new monthly payment of $[New Amount] will be due on [Due Date] beginning [Month/Year].

>
> Thank you for being a valued resident.
>
> Sincerely,
> [Property Manager Name]
> [Company]

### 10 — Move-Out Instructions

> **MOVE-OUT INSTRUCTIONS — [Address, Unit #]**
>
> Move-Out Date: [Date]
> Move-Out Inspection: [Date] at [Time]
>
> **To ensure the return of your full security deposit, please complete the following:**
>
> ✅ Clean all appliances (oven, refrigerator, dishwasher, microwave)
> ✅ Clean all floors, carpets vacuumed
> ✅ Wipe down all surfaces, counters, sinks, toilets, tubs/showers
> ✅ Remove all personal belongings from unit and storage
> ✅ Patch any nail holes with spackle (small holes only — large damage is chargeable)
> ✅ Return all keys, parking passes, and access cards
> ✅ Provide a forwarding address for security deposit disposition
> ✅ Cancel or transfer all utilities effective [Move-Out Date]
>
> **Security Deposit:**
> Your deposit of $[Amount] will be returned within [X days] of move-out, less any deductions for damages beyond normal wear and tear, outstanding rent, or cleaning fees. An itemized statement will be provided.
>
> Thank you for your tenancy.
> [Property Manager Name] | [Phone]
