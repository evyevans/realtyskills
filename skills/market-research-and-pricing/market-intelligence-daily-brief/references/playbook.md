# Market Intelligence Brief — detailed procedure

## Market and source scope

Confirm geographic boundary, property segment, tenure/transaction type, reporting frequency, and actual as-of date. Use authorized listing-system exports, official statistics, land registry or other applicable public sources, and original lender/central-bank information where needed. Do not claim live MLS or proprietary portal access without a tool and permission. Distinguish source publication date, data period, and retrieval date.

A daily brief may contain weekly/monthly statistics; label the frequency rather than imply those numbers changed today. Separate current listings snapshots from completed transactions and lagged registry data. Note seasonally adjusted versus unadjusted data, preliminary/revised releases, sample size, and geography changes.

## Metric table

For prices, identify median/mean, segment, transaction sample, period, and whether mix changes could explain movement. For sales, inventory, days-on-market, new listings, reductions, or list/sale ratios, use consistent status/field definitions. Show count and denominator where available.

Percentage change = (current − prior) / prior when prior is nonzero. Rate differences should distinguish percentage points from relative percentage changes. Months of supply = active inventory / average monthly sales over a defined compatible period; zero sales makes it undefined. Do not classify the market from fixed inventory or DOM cutoffs.

Financing context needs rate product, lender/source, quoted date, fees/points, and any relevant borrower assumptions. A policy rate is not a consumer mortgage quote. Do not invent a local mortgage rate, trend, or affordability conclusion.

## Interpretation

Separate facts, plausible explanations, and scenarios. Consider seasonality, stock mix, transaction lag, and sample limits. Provide practical next questions for listing, buyer, and investor conversations only where relevant. Do not forecast price direction, promised transaction volume, or a “best time to buy” from one indicator. Unverified current data requires a clearly labeled supplied-data brief and an exact research list.

## Deliverable

Return as-of and source freshness note; concise executive brief; compatible-period metric/change table; supported observations and alternative explanations; agent implications; plain-language client talking points; and source/data gaps. Keep source links adjacent to claims and indicate anything that could not be independently verified. Optional CRM/newsletter distribution requires actual user authorization.

## Before delivery

All statistics have geography, segment, period, definition, and source; comparison periods match; daily wording does not imply daily data; policy and mortgage rates are distinct; no manufactured forecast.
