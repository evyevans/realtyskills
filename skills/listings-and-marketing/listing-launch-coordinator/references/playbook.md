# Listing Launch Plan — detailed procedure

## Intake and dependencies

Confirm signed listing authority/status, seller-approved public facts, occupancy/showing constraints, repairs, staging goals, access arrangements, vendors, approved budget, existing assets, and desired launch. Work backward using actual availability and production lead times, then test feasibility forward. Do not impose a universal photography buffer or promise a compressed schedule. Label proposed dates and replan around unresolved dependencies.

## Preparation and assets

Create property-specific seller tasks for access, cleaning, safety, visible maintenance, decluttering, and approved presentation. Avoid hiding material defects. Repairs need quotes, access, permits where relevant, and actual approval; no invented repair ROI. Identify disclosures and factual verification before publication.

Write a photographer brief covering property type, verified features, requested channels/aspect ratios, shot priorities, realistic access, privacy, licensing, editing boundaries, and delivery needs. Do not prescribe a fixed photo count or misrepresent space through edits. Virtual staging or generated imagery requires accurate labeling under applicable rules; factual photos and conceptual images remain distinct. Drone work depends on qualified providers and applicable permissions.

## Launch materials and schedule

Create complete headline/listing draft, launch caption, announcement email, and showing/open-house copy for requested channels. Use actual event details; leave an event out if none exists. Check current listing-system and brokerage rules for coming-soon, public marketing, availability, disclosures, and required identifiers. Confirm image/music rights and consent for identifiable people.

Schedule publication and seller updates within authorized channels and budget. Do not assume a database is consented, all channels are connected, or a public post is permitted before listing activation. Sending/publishing is conditional on actual authority and tool availability; mark drafts and scheduling proposals accurately.

## Performance review

Build a tracker for source-defined views, inquiries, showings, feedback, offers, and relevant competing listings. Define periods and denominators; metrics from different portals are not directly comparable. Review against current local segment evidence and seller goals rather than universal views/showings counts. Low activity may indicate exposure, access, condition, price, or data issues. Recommend a diagnostic discussion before any price change; do not trigger automatic reductions.

## Deliverable

Return launch readiness/blocker summary; dated or relative dependency table with owners; seller prep checklist; photography/asset brief; finished marketing drafts; permitted-channel deployment plan; showings/event logistics if applicable; budget/approval register; and a performance review tracker with conditional actions. Unknown availability or costs remain explicit.

## Before delivery

Launch dates are feasible under actual dependencies, defects are not concealed, assets/status/disclosures are verified, spending stays within real authority, and performance thresholds are locally grounded.
