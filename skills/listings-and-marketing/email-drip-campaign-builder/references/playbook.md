# Email Campaign Plan — detailed procedure

## Audience and eligibility

Distinguish requested service updates from marketing and mixed-purpose messages using actual purpose. Confirm jurisdiction, contact source, applicable lawful basis/consent rules, suppression flags, preferred frequency, existing campaigns, and sender identity. Do not assume a past client or public listing is eligible for every campaign. If eligibility is unresolved, deliver review drafts and a hold register; do not enroll contacts.

## Sequence design

Choose a requested number/horizon or propose a manageable relative schedule explicitly for approval. Each email should answer a real audience question, give supported value, and invite one voluntary action. Do not diagnose “audience psychology” from protected traits or personal vulnerabilities. Use current factual educational material and user-approved services, not invented inventory, secret buyers, fake testimonials, guaranteed appreciation, or fabricated valuation reports.

For each email provide subject, preview text if used, full body, CTA, recipient/trigger conditions, timing basis, merge fields, and required sender/footer items. Respect actual brand voice and language. Placeholder sender address or unsubscribe mechanism must be labeled if missing; do not claim they are configured. Required content differs by local law and platform.

## Branch and stop logic

A reply creates a human/task handoff unless an approved response branch exists. Stop or pause on opt-out, do-not-contact, incorrect address, permanent bounce, changed status, goal completion, complaint, or representation/contact restriction as applicable. Check campaign collisions and duplicate enrollment. Nonresponse does not justify unlimited escalation; clicks/opens can be unreliable and do not establish consent or verified intent.

Define proposed automation using clear trigger → eligibility check → action → stop/exception rows. Apply actual CRM fields and behavior only when known. Do not invent tags, webhook schemas, installed automations, or tool capabilities. For authorization to deploy, verify account, list, suppression processing, merge-field preview, sender/footer setup, and result. Avoid live “test” sends to real recipients without scope authorization.

## Deliverable

Return audience/eligibility brief; full email sequence; trigger/cadence table; complete reply templates for likely branches; suppression/stop matrix; merge-field mapping and missing values; and import/deployment checklist. Without a connected platform, provide usable copy and configuration instructions in chat; no claim of campaign activation.

## Before delivery

All emails are fully written, subjects and claims are truthful, cadence is proposed or approved, opt-outs override enrollment, and footer/merge fields are verified or precisely flagged.

## Official starting points

[FTC commercial-email guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business) for U.S. commercial email. Verify the applicable privacy/communications authority and platform rules for other locations.

These are jurisdiction-specific starting points, not universal rules or proof of current applicability. Open and verify the relevant source during use; choose the corresponding local authority elsewhere.
