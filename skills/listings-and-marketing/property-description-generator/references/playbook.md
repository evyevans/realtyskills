# Property Listing Copy — detailed procedure

## Fact intake

Use supplied listing sheets, approved documents, or authorized property records. Confirm property type, size/units and measurement source, rooms, condition, features, price if publishing, availability, tenure, parking, outdoor areas, accessibility features, and any restrictions relevant to the requested copy. Ask for publishing limits and required brokerage identifiers when needed. Names, exact address, price, and market are optional if the user requests an unbranded draft.

Do not infer floor area, legal bedroom count, recent renovation, permitted use, rental income, views, school quality, safety, or distances from incomplete notes. Photos can support visible details but do not prove permits, materials, dimensions, structural condition, or off-camera amenities. Flag inconsistent inputs rather than writing the more marketable claim.

## Positioning and writing

Build a feature-to-benefit list that describes the property itself. A flexible room may offer space for different uses only if supported; do not label occupants by family status, age, religion, or another protected trait. Avoid steering through demographic descriptions, “ideal buyer” restrictions, coded exclusivity, or unsupported neighborhood judgments. Use measured accessibility details rather than promising full accessibility from one feature.

Draft headline options grounded in distinguishing facts. Write listing copy with an opening feature, logical walkthrough, practical property details, and accurate viewing/contact CTA. Extended website copy may add verified details rather than repeat adjectives. Social variants should suit the requested channel and asset; email includes subject, preview text, body, and CTA. Do not invent open-house events, price changes, scarcity, testimonials, “investment opportunity” yields, or claims that a property is available if status is unknown.

## Format and checks

Count characters against the user's actual listing-system limit, stating whether spaces and line breaks are counted. Use code if available; otherwise describe the count as approximate. Do not prescribe universal platform limits or hashtag counts. Verify current official platform rules when requested. Required legal and brokerage disclosures depend on local rules and user-approved identity information; flag those missing rather than inventing them.

## Deliverable

Provide feature/claim register with verification status, a small set of meaningful headlines, complete listing copy with count, complete extended copy, requested captions with visual direction and factual CTA, and the email version. Include only brief publication holds for unverified claims or missing required disclosures. Do not publish or stage a live listing unless authorized.

## Before delivery

Every factual benefit traces to supplied evidence; no buyer stereotypes, unsupported location claims, invented events, artificial scarcity, or implied professional inspections. Actual channel limits are honored.

## Official starting points

[HUD housing rights and obligations](https://www.hud.gov/stat/fheo/rights-obligations) for relevant U.S. fair-housing issues; use the applicable local housing/advertising authority elsewhere.

These are jurisdiction-specific starting points, not universal rules or proof of current applicability. Open and verify the relevant source during use; choose the corresponding local authority elsewhere.
