# Social Media Content Plan — detailed procedure

## Strategy and feasibility

Choose themes tied to the user's actual services and audience questions: property features, process education, sourced market context, professional practice, or authorized client experience. Use the user's objective and available production resources, not fixed “optimal” percentages, weekday themes, or holiday assumptions. Confirm a dated horizon or propose a clearly labeled relative schedule. Seasonal references must fit the actual location and reporting date.

## Evidence and creative production

Maintain a claim register for prices, statistics, property status, experience, results, testimonials, rates, deadlines, and offers. Use current sources where relevant. Never invent a successful transaction, client quote, local event, open house, buyer demand, or trend. Client stories require permission and privacy review. Distinguish real property imagery from conceptual/AI-generated material; do not alter factual marketing imagery to hide defects or depict nonexistent amenities.

For each calendar item create: audience question, purpose, verified factual basis, format, complete caption or spoken script, on-screen text if useful, visual shot/design brief, asset source/rights, CTA, owner, and review dependencies. Video briefs should specify opening, sequence, dialogue/narration, and closing, without promising a particular duration or performance unless scoped. Include alt-text guidance where it helps accessibility.

## Channel adaptation

Adapt copy to the user's requested channel and current actual limits; verify official specifications when needed. Do not assume universal caption length, hashtag count, trending audio, best posting hour, or algorithm performance. Hashtags are relevant descriptors rather than guaranteed discovery tools. If no channel is specified, produce neutral copy that can be adapted and ask only when a platform-specific constraint matters.

Use property-focused inclusive language. Paid housing audience rules and required advertising disclosures differ from organic-copy requirements; flag unverified targeting settings separately. Do not promise reach, leads, engagement, or sales. Avoid engagement bait, fake urgency, unlicensed media, and invented brand achievements.

## Deployment and learning

Provide a review gate for factual claims, assets/consents, and required local identity/disclosures. For authorized scheduling, verify account, time zone, asset, caption, and tool result. Without tools, return the full drafts and import-ready calendar in chat or an editable file when possible.

Define objective-linked metrics and periods (such as qualified inquiries, link visits, or production completion), with clear denominators and limitations. Compare actual outcomes later; no fabricated baseline or conversion forecast.

## Deliverable

Return concise strategy; content/claim inventory; feasible calendar; complete drafts/scripts; visual and asset briefs; review/permission checklist; and a measurement table. The user should be able to produce or approve each item without asking the agent to write the missing copy.

## Before delivery

Every calendar slot has finished copy and actionable asset direction; statistics/events/status are sourced; schedule matches capacity; permissions and factual versus conceptual imagery are explicit.
