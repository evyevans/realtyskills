# Motivated Seller Outreach — detailed procedure

## Contact and context

Confirm whether the person asked for contact, was referred with permission, or is an eligible outbound prospect. Determine which local solicitation, calling, texting, email, platform, and representation rules apply. A public listing, public phone number, expired listing, or “for sale by owner” status does not establish consent for every channel. Honor do-not-contact and suppression records. If eligibility is uncertain, mark outreach on hold and provide a review draft rather than enrollment or send instructions.

Use only stated goals and actual property facts. Do not infer divorce, bereavement, financial need, vulnerability, or urgency from ownership or court records. Clarify the sender's actual role (agent, broker, investor, or another role), purpose, and any conflict. Do not claim a cash buyer, an existing offer, market-specific expertise, free services, or a valuation already completed unless supported.

## Scripts

For a call: identify sender and purpose, ask whether a brief conversation is welcome, acknowledge only confirmed context, ask about the owner's objectives, offer a relevant real service, and propose one voluntary next step. Include a voicemail alternative without private details.

For text: use concise identity/purpose and a suitable reply or opt-out path under applicable requirements. For email: provide subject, preview where useful, full body, clear CTA, and required sender/footer items with precise missing-field markers. Do not specify a universal message count, call hour, response deadline, or drip cadence.

Create branches for interest, timing uncertainty, a price question, existing representation, wrong person, refusal, and opt-out. If price discussion is requested, offer a sourced comparison or ask for necessary evidence rather than quote a guessed value. Avoid rebuttals that minimize legal rights or pressure a distressed person.

## Follow-up and output

Follow-ups depend on the person's response, preferences, lawful basis, and user-approved policy. A decline or opt-out stops outreach; nonresponse is not consent. Do not switch channels to bypass restrictions.

Return contact-basis status; fact/claim register; complete scripts for requested eligible channels; discovery questions; reply branches; proposed follow-up/stop logic; and an appointment confirmation draft only when the appointment details exist. Scheduling and sending occur only with the user's authorization and an available tool.

## Before delivery

No inferred motivation, fake buyer, fabricated property valuation, implied government affiliation, unapproved service promise, or automatic follow-up. Restricted records remain excluded.

## Official starting points

[FTC commercial-email guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business) for applicable U.S. email; consult the relevant communications/privacy regulator for calls, texts, and other jurisdictions.

These are jurisdiction-specific starting points, not universal rules or proof of current applicability. Open and verify the relevant source during use; choose the corresponding local authority elsewhere.
