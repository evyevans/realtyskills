# Lead Qualification — detailed procedure

## Intake and contact boundaries

Use aliases or stable references. A full name, address, phone, or financial record is unnecessary unless the requested action needs it. For each record capture role, actual inquiry, source if operationally useful, supplied timeline/date, last confirmed interaction, requested next step, assigned owner, representation status if known, and channel eligibility. Unknown data remains unknown. A contact source does not establish consent or intent.

Opt-outs and do-not-contact flags override every priority or stage. Mark restricted records excluded from outreach; do not switch channels to evade a preference. If local channel eligibility is unresolved, prepare drafts on hold and specify what to verify. Existing representation or a required intermediary changes the communication path; do not recommend inducing breach.

## Readiness and discovery

For buyers, organize stated property needs, search area, budget/currency if supplied and relevant, desired timing, viewing needs, and self-reported lender/financing status. A pre-approval is not a guarantee of funds, affordability, or closing. Missing financing information suggests an appropriate discovery question or lender handoff, not denial of service.

For sellers, organize verified property facts, stated objectives/timing, current marketing or representation, information needed for a CMA, and title/occupancy questions where relevant. Do not infer equity or motivation from ownership duration, location, appearance, family circumstances, or public distress records. For linked buy/sell needs, identify dependencies and separate readiness on each side rather than automatically prioritizing them because of potential fees.

## Prioritization

Default to an explainable action queue based on actual requests, unanswered service commitments, stated deadlines, and agreed follow-up. Describe the evidence behind each placement; ties can retain input order. Missing data requires discovery, not a negative prediction. Contact-source prestige, inferred wealth, protected traits, personal vulnerability, and stereotypes must not determine access to service or housing.

If numeric scoring is requested, use a transparent user-approved heuristic limited to task-relevant workflow signals. State dimensions, weights, thresholds, treatment of missing data, and scope before interpreting totals. Show component evidence and mark an incomplete score provisional; do not silently score unknown as low intent. No universal source ranking, conversion forecast, financing-eligibility verdict, or “not qualified” label. Any local benchmark needs actual data and explicit limitations.

## Actions and drafts

For each eligible record identify the next useful action, responsible person/role, confirmed or proposed timing, precise missing input, and reason. Write a complete message when requested or useful: truthful sender identity, reference to the actual inquiry, answer or relevant question, and a voluntary next step. Do not claim a property list, valuation, appointment, or lender check exists unless it was actually supplied or completed.

Apply user-approved contact/cadence policies and stop on refusal, opt-out, wrong contact, completed goal, or changed preference. Do not auto-enroll, archive, downgrade, or assign people merely because a score suggests it. Live CRM updates/sends require the user's applicable authorization.

## Deliverable

Return scope/records reviewed; excluded/held contact summary with minimum private detail; prioritized table (reference, role/request, evidence, status, missing information, next action, owner, timing); buyer/seller readiness and discovery notes; optional scoring components if requested; full message drafts; and unresolved facts/verification tasks. Do not project transaction probabilities, commission value, or pipeline revenue from budgets.

## Before delivery

Priorities are explained by actual service needs; opt-outs override actions; unknown information is not a bad lead score; readiness facts are not eligibility decisions; no invented history, financial qualification, or conversion forecast.
