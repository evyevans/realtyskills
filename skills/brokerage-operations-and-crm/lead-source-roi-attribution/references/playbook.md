# Lead Source ROI — detailed procedure

## Data boundaries and attribution

Choose cohort-based conversion (leads acquired in a defined period followed through outcomes) or period activity, and disclose lag. Do not mix current spend with unrelated historic closings without explanation. Reconcile unique lead/transaction IDs, duplicate imports, referrals, direct/unknown sources, and cancellations. Define source taxonomy and selected first-touch, last-touch, or fractional model. Fractional revenue weights must sum to one per transaction unless unallocated credit is explicitly shown.

Use net revenue actually retained by the business after agreed splits/referral arrangements, or clearly label gross commission instead. Do not calculate revenue from lead budgets, a standard commission rate, or presumed transaction value. Separate media spend, agency/platform costs, staff handling costs, transaction costs, and overhead treatment. Do not allocate the same costs twice.

## Calculations

CPL = applicable source acquisition spend / unique attributable leads. Stage conversion = number progressing / defined eligible cohort at the prior stage; state denominator and observation window. Acquisition cost per customer/closed transaction = defined acquisition costs / attributable outcomes. Zero outcomes yields no finite acquisition-cost figure; zero leads makes CPL undefined.

ROAS = attributable revenue / advertising spend; it is not net profit. ROI = (attributable retained revenue − all included attributable costs) / included attributable costs, with cost basis shown. Zero costs makes that ROI undefined, not infinite by default. Distinguish contribution margin from net profit where overhead is excluded. Break-even and payback calculations need timing and margin data, not revenue alone.

## Interpretation and scenarios

Compare volume, qualified outcomes, lag, consistency, customer fit based on service needs, operational capacity, and data confidence. Small samples or an unobserved sales cycle limit ranking. Do not imply causal lift from simple attribution or use unsupported industry conversion benchmarks. Unknown-source revenue belongs in an explicit bucket.

If allocation is requested, preserve the total budget, contractual commitments, handling capacity, and user-approved constraints. Build a scenario with stated marginal-cost/conversion assumptions; historic average performance does not prove spending scales linearly. Suggest a bounded experiment with success criteria and observation window chosen for the business, not a universal reallocation percentage. No advertising spend or campaign change without authorization.

## Deliverable

Return scope/cohort/attribution definitions; reconciliation notes; source table with spend, funnel outcomes, retained revenue, costs and labeled metrics; lag/sample-size warnings; evidence-backed observations; user-target comparison; optional allocation/experiment scenarios; and data questions that could change the conclusion.

## Before delivery

Periods/cohorts align; fractional revenue is not counted twice; revenue is actual or labeled; ROI and ROAS remain distinct; zero denominators and missing costs are disclosed; budgets reconcile.
