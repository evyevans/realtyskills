# Agent Recruiting Machine

The brokerage that wins on recruiting wins the market. This skill gives brokerage owners and team leads the exact words to attract, qualify, and close top agents — from first contact to signed agreement.

## When to Use

- Brokerage owner building out a recruiting pipeline
- Team lead targeting productive agents at competing brokerages
- Brokerage adding recruiting to their systematic growth strategy
- Preparing for recruiting conversations with specific agents

## OUTPUT 1 — Cold Outreach Scripts

### Phone / Cold Call Script

> "Hi, is this [Agent Name]? Hey [Name], this is [Your Name], I'm the [Owner/Manager] at [Brokerage]. I know you're probably busy, so I'll keep this quick — I've been following your production in [Market] and you've been doing really solid work. I'd love to have a 15-minute conversation to share what we're building over here and see if there's any reason it might be worth a conversation. Is that something you'd be open to?"

**If they say "I'm happy where I am":**
> "I totally respect that — and honestly, that's exactly who I want to talk to. The agents who are content are usually the ones who have standards. All I'm asking for is 15 minutes — if there's nothing there, no hard feelings. Would [Day/Time] work?"

**If they say "what's your split?":**
> "Great question — I'd rather walk you through the full economics so you can compare apples to apples. Can I send you a quick comparison? Most agents are surprised when they see what they're actually netting after fees."

**If they say "send me information":**
> "I'll send something over today. And just so I send the right thing — what's the one thing you'd most want to see change about your current situation?"

## OUTPUT 2 — Recruiting Email Sequence (5-Touch)

| Touch | Timing | Goal |
|-------|--------|------|
| 1 | Day 0 | Introduction + curiosity |
| 2 | Day 4 | Value-add — specific differentiator |
| 3 | Day 9 | Social proof — agent story or result |
| 4 | Day 16 | Overcome "happy where I am" objection |
| 5 | Day 25 | Final reach — leave door open |

**Touch 2 — Value-Add Email:**
> Subject: What most agents don't know they're giving up
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Quick follow-up — I wanted to share something specific about what we offer that I don't think most agents realize they're missing at a traditional brokerage.
>
> [Specific value prop in 2–3 sentences — e.g., "We generate an average of [X] leads per agent per month through our [program]. Our agents focus on converting — not prospecting from scratch."]
>
> Happy to walk you through the specifics in a short call. Are you free [Day] or [Day]?
>
> [Your Name]

**Touch 3 — Social Proof Email:**
> Subject: What [Agent Name] said after 6 months with us
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> I wanted to share a quick note from one of our agents, [Agent Name], who joined us [X months] ago from [Competitor Brokerage]:
>
> *"[Genuine quote from agent about their experience — production improvement, culture, support, income, etc.]"*
>
> [Agent Name] did [$X / X deals] in their first [X months] with us. It's not for everyone — but for the right agent, the difference is significant.
>
> Would you be open to a quick call this week?
>
> [Your Name]

**Touch 4 — Objection Pre-empt Email:**
> Subject: "I'm happy where I am" — I hear this a lot
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> The most common thing I hear from great agents is "I'm happy where I am." I get it — changing brokerages feels like a big decision and you've built something where you are.
>
> But here's what I've noticed: the agents who talk to us and decide to stay where they are always say the same thing — "I'm glad I at least had the conversation." And the ones who do join almost always say they wish they'd made the move sooner.
>
> I'm not asking you to decide anything. Just talk.
>
> 15 minutes. Anytime this week. Reply and I'll send you a link.
>
> [Your Name]

**Touch 5 — Final Email:**
> Subject: Leaving the door open
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> I've reached out a few times — I respect that now may not be the right timing. I won't keep following up after this.
>
> If things ever change — whether it's your split, your support, your team, or just a feeling that it's time for something different — I'd welcome a conversation. [Brokerage] will likely look different in [6/12] months, and the door is open.
>
> You know where to find me.
>
> [Your Name] | [Phone]

## OUTPUT 3 — Value Proposition Builder

Use this framework to articulate what makes your brokerage worth switching for. Be specific — vague claims don't close agents.

**Weak:** "We offer great support."
**Strong:** "We have a full-time TC who handles all paperwork for every transaction — our agents spend their time on leads and clients, not contracts."

**Weak:** "We have better splits."
**Strong:** "Our agents cap at $[X] and go to 100% commission for the rest of the year. An agent doing [$X] volume typically nets [$X] more with us than at [Competitor]."

**Template — Fill In Your Specifics:**

> At [Brokerage], here's what agents actually get:
>
> 💰 **Economics:** [Split structure, cap, fees — be transparent]
>
> 📞 **Leads:** [Specific lead program, average per agent per month, conversion support]
>
> 🛠️ **Support:** [TC support, marketing team, showing agents, coordinator]
>
> 🧠 **Training:** [Weekly meetings, coaching, accountability structure, CE credits]
>
> 💻 **Technology:** [CRM, transaction management, showing scheduler, marketing platform]
>
> 🏆 **Culture:** [Team size, collaborative vs. competitive, leadership access, events]
>
> 📍 **Brand:** [Local recognition, ranking, specialization, referral network]

## OUTPUT 4 — Interview / Discovery Questions

Use these in your recruiting meeting to uncover what the agent actually needs — not what you assume they need.

**Career & Motivation:**
- "Tell me about your business right now — what's working and what's frustrating you?"
- "If you could change one thing about your current brokerage, what would it be?"
- "Where do you want to be in 3 years — production-wise, income-wise, lifestyle-wise?"
- "What made you take this meeting today?"

**Production & Business:**
- "What was your volume / units last year?"
- "Where does most of your business come from — referrals, leads, sphere, farming?"
- "How much of your time goes to admin vs. client-facing work?"
- "What systems or tools are you using today? What's missing?"

**Decision-Making:**
- "If you were to make a move, what would need to be true for you to feel confident about it?"
- "Is there anyone else who would factor into this decision — a spouse, a partner agent?"
- "What's your timeline for making a decision? Are you actively looking, or just exploring?"

**Compensation:**
- "Walk me through your current economics — I want to make sure we're comparing apples to apples."
- "Do you know what you net after splits, fees, and expenses? Have you ever mapped it out?"

## Recruiting Pipeline Tracker (Guidance)

Organize recruiting contacts into stages:

| Stage | Definition |
|-------|-----------|
| **Awareness** | Agent identified, not yet contacted |
| **Outreach** | First contact made, awaiting response |
| **Engaged** | Agent responded, conversation started |
| **Interview** | Meeting scheduled or completed |
| **Offer** | Terms discussed, decision pending |
| **Closed** | Signed and onboarding |
| **Nurture** | Not ready now — follow up in 90 days |

Note: The best recruiting candidates are rarely actively looking. Build relationships before they're ready — then be the first call when they are.
