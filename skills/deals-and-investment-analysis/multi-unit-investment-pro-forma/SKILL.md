# Multifamily Pro Forma

Prepare an auditable multifamily or multi-unit property pro forma from a rent roll, operating history, capital plan, and funding terms. Use when comparing current and stabilized performance, equity cash flows, exits, and sensitivities.

## Inputs and result

**Provide:** Unit-level rent roll, matching-period operating statements/collections, leases, capital and lease-up schedule, price, financing, equity terms if relevant, hold period, and exit assumptions.

**Deliver:** Reconciled rent roll/T12, current and stabilized NOI, debt and capital model, periodic equity cash flows, supported return metrics, sensitivity matrix, and investor brief.

**Applicability:** Global workflow; verify local rules, market evidence, and user-specific terms

## Core workflow

1. Reconcile unit counts, leases, collection periods, operating statements, and capital requirements.
2. Separate in-place performance from conditional lease-up and renovation projections.
3. Model actual debt, capital funding, timing, and any agreed distribution terms.
4. Calculate periodic cash flow, net exit proceeds, and returns from consistent cash flows.
5. Deliver the pro forma, sensitivities, risk/evidence register, and clearly conditional investment summary.
