# Wholesale Deal Analysis

Underwrite a proposed wholesale real estate transaction using documented valuation, repair, funding, and assignment terms. Use when comparing an acquisition price, end-buyer economics, and alternative exit scenarios.

## Inputs and result

**Provide:** Property and jurisdiction, evidence for resale value, contract/assignment terms, repair scope and quotes, acquisition costs, funding terms, exit costs, and investor targets.

**Deliver:** ARV evidence, repair budget, end-buyer and wholesaler economics, maximum-offer worksheet, exit comparison, funding needs, and diligence questions.

**Applicability:** Global workflow; verify local rules, market evidence, and user-specific terms

## Core workflow

1. Confirm the contract interest, local wholesale restrictions, occupancy, and evidence available.
2. Estimate resale value from relevant closed sales and stated renovation assumptions.
3. Build sourced repair, closing, financing, holding, and exit cost schedules.
4. Compare wholesale and any requested alternative exits without double-counting fees or financing.
5. Deliver conditional offer economics, liquidity requirements, risk flags, and a factual seller-facing summary.
