# Rental Property Evaluation

Build a transparent rental-property underwriting model from actual income, expenses, financing, and investor assumptions. Use when evaluating NOI, debt coverage, cash flow, liquidity, and hold/exit scenarios.

## Inputs and result

**Provide:** Price/currency, rent and occupancy evidence, operating expenses, capital/reserve plan, loan terms, acquisition costs, hold period, and explicit growth/exit assumptions.

**Deliver:** Income/expense schedules, NOI and debt metrics, after-reserve cash flow, investment basis, hold/exit model, sensitivities, and evidence-based decision conditions.

**Applicability:** Global workflow; verify local rules, market evidence, and user-specific terms

## Core workflow

1. Reconcile rents, occupancy, expenses, financing, and the periods represented.
2. Build income and operating expenses with capital costs and financing separated.
3. Calculate debt service, NOI, cap rate, DSCR, equity cash flow, and defined cash-on-cash return.
4. Project only supplied or explicitly modeled assumptions, including capital needs and net exit proceeds.
5. Deliver an auditable model, downside scenarios, funding gaps, and comparison to the user’s targets.
