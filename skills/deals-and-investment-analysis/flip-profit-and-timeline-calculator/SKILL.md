# Fix and Flip Analysis

Model a real estate renovation-and-resale project with transparent costs, funding, timing, and downside scenarios. Use when evaluating projected flip profit, liquidity, break-even resale price, and construction dependencies.

## Inputs and result

**Provide:** Acquisition and resale evidence, renovation scope/quotes, permits, financing and draw terms, transaction costs, holding expenses, actual milestones, and return targets.

**Deliver:** Itemized project budget, funding/cash-flow schedule, profit and return metrics, break-even resale calculation, dependency timeline, and sensitivity report.

**Applicability:** Global workflow; verify local rules, market evidence, and user-specific terms

## Core workflow

1. Validate resale evidence, scope, funding terms, project dates, and missing cost categories.
2. Build the acquisition, renovation, carrying, financing, and disposition budgets.
3. Model cash contributions, loan draws/payments, and net sale proceeds consistently.
4. Calculate profit, defined returns, and break-even value; compare approved downside assumptions.
5. Deliver the budget, cash timing, critical-path plan, scenarios, and decision conditions.
