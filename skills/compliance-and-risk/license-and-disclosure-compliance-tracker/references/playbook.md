# License and Disclosure Tracker — detailed procedure

## Requirements register

For each requirement record jurisdiction, affected role/transaction, official source, version/effective period, trigger, deadline/counting rule, required evidence, and verification status. Use the applicable licensing regulator, statutes, official forms, and any relevant national/local authority. Do not invent CE hours, renewal fees, grace periods, disclosure exceptions, file-retention periods, insurance limits, or a renewal window from remembered practice.

## License and education reconciliation

Record license identifier only when needed, type, affiliation, status as reported by the official source, verification date, expiration, renewal window if verified, education categories/hours, and submitted/accepted status. A valid-looking certificate does not establish regulator acceptance. Match certificates to the actual reporting cycle and approved provider/category; flag duplicates or out-of-cycle credits. For each category, remaining requirement = verified requirement minus accepted applicable completion, bounded at zero without transferring surplus across categories unless allowed.

Calculate days to expiration from the actual report date in the relevant time zone. Use user-selected reminder lead times, not universal color thresholds. Distinguish overdue action from a verified expired license. If official status cannot be checked, label it unverified and request the relevant record.

## Disclosure and brokerage tasks

For each transaction identify role, property/use, document set, conditions that determine applicability, required form/version, recipient, delivery/acknowledgment standard, triggering event, due date, evidence location, owner, and next task. Do not label a checklist “universally required.” Federal, local, transaction, and property exemptions must be evaluated separately.

If brokerage scope is requested, review documented supervision, trust/escrow reconciliation, insurance, registrations, advertising identity, affiliated-license status, and retention obligations against verified sources. Missing evidence is an unresolved item, not proof of violation. Do not recommend purging records without confirmed retention requirements and user authorization.

## Deliverable

Return requirements/source register; license and education reconciliation; disclosure applicability/delivery matrix; calendar of sourced deadlines plus labeled proposed reminders; urgency based on verified dates/consequences; evidence gaps; and assigned actions. If submission tools are available and authorized, verify portal target, fields, and result; do not certify licensing status or pay fees automatically.

## Before delivery

Every asserted requirement has a current applicable source; dates use confirmed triggers; course hours are counted once in the proper category/cycle; official status and evidence status remain separate.
