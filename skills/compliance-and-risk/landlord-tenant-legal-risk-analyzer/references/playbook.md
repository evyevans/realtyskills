# Landlord and Tenant Risk Review — detailed procedure

## Facts and chronology

Confirm location down to the level governing the issue, property type, tenancy status, subsidized/regulated context if relevant, role, lease version/addenda, occupancy and possession events, payments, repair requests, notices, and service evidence. Use aliases unless formal drafting needs identities. Distinguish allegations, firsthand records, and unresolved conflicts. Do not label conduct criminal or a lease violation from one assertion.

## Issue analysis

Identify applicable topics: rent/payment dispute, lease obligations, habitability/repairs, access, tenancy ending, deposit accounting, accommodation, discrimination, retaliation, or another matter. Do not classify a breach as legally curable/incurable without verified law and facts. Check national/regional/local requirements, regulations, applicable emergency measures, and official court or housing-authority guidance. A generic state summary is insufficient when city-level rules control.

Record each issue with lease section, event/evidence, rule/source and verification status, missing facts, operational consequence, and counsel question. Distinguish repair obligations from liability for damage; do not recommend withholding repairs to pressure payment. Accommodation/assistance-animal and protected-activity issues need contextual review rather than generic pet or late-rent rules.

## Notices and deadlines

Calculate a deadline only after identifying the applicable trigger, delivery/service requirements, period, counting convention, local holidays, extensions/exceptions, and source. Do not insert a universal notice duration, deposit-return period, retaliation window, or penalty multiple. Without those facts, provide the notice/deadline worksheet and precise questions.

Do not advise lockouts, utility shutoff, property seizure, removal of occupants, or court filing from a generic skill. Prepare a neutral factual communication if useful; formal legal notices require verified applicable content and qualified local review before service. Determine whether the user is asking for drafting versus actual delivery; never claim service occurred from an email draft.

## Deposit and account reconciliation

Build an itemized ledger of receipts, credits, payments, deposit held, proposed deductions, supporting invoices/photos, documented condition, and amounts in dispute. Separate normal wear/damage questions for local review; do not resolve them from an unsupported accusation. Check arithmetic and distinguish rent/deposit categories. Do not quote damages or penalties without applicable current authority and established facts.

## Deliverable

Return scope and chronology; evidence register; prioritized issue/source matrix; reconciled ledger; sourced or unresolved deadline table; urgent safety/repair routing where applicable; neutral draft communications; documentation tasks; and focused housing-authority/legal-aid/counsel questions appropriate to the user's role. Avoid a definitive eviction, liability, or enforceability conclusion.

## Before delivery

Local applicability, lease version, actual service evidence, counting rule, and disputed facts stay explicit. No universal notice periods or self-help actions; deposits and rent reconcile separately.
